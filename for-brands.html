BuyBritish helps UK shoppers find products from British brands and see, at a glance, how British each one is. This page explains how your products appear here and what we do and don't do.

How BuyBritish works

Shoppers browse or search by category on this site and in our mobile app, which is in development. Each product shows its brand, its price, a medal, and a link to buy it from the retailer. We don't sell anything ourselves and never handle orders, payment or delivery: every purchase happens on your site.

The medal system

"British" can mean several things: where a company is based, or where a product is designed, assembled or made. The medal makes that distinction visible instead of blurring it.

Gold · Made in Britain

Made in Britain, by a brand headquartered in Britain.

Silver · British brand or British-made

Either a British brand that makes abroad, or a global brand that makes in Britain.

Bronze · Assembled in Britain

A global brand that assembles the product in Britain.

Products that don't meet any of these aren't given a medal.

How we check

Brand claimed means the medal rests on the brand's own published statement about where it is based and where it makes its products. We link to that statement on the page.

Verified means we have confirmed it independently, for example through a certification scheme, a checked factory address or a Companies House record.

Every medal on the site today is Brand claimed. We show the status next to each brand so shoppers know exactly what a medal rests on.

Brands can't pay for a medal, for a higher medal, or for Verified status. A medal depends only on where the brand is based and where the product is made.

How partner products appear

  • Your own data. Titles, images and prices come from the product feed you publish through Awin. We don't rewrite your descriptions or alter your prices.
  • In stock only. Products your feed marks as out of stock aren't shown.
  • One listing per product. Sizes and colours are grouped into a single listing showing the lowest current price.
  • Dated prices. Each category page states when prices were last checked, and says that delivery is extra where your feed doesn't give a delivery cost.
  • Straight to your site. Every listing links to the product page on your own site through your Awin tracking link.
  • Medals by brand, not by retailer. If you stock several brands, each one is assessed separately. Brands we haven't checked yet aren't listed.

Product links are marked as sponsored, and commission rates play no part in which medal a product receives.
