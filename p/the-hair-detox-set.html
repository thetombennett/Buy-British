The Hair Detox Set is a three-step system created to support your transition to our natural haircare and restore lasting scalp balance, and health. Combining deep cleansing, gentle nourishment, and essential moisture, it helps reset your hair from root to tip — without sulphates, silicones, or synthetic foaming agents. The Hair Detox Set is ideal for: Those starting their journey to natural haircare or as a reset if your hair ever feels heavy or coated (e.g post salon visit).
