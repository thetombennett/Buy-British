Designed to enhance your outdoor experience, the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit is an innovative piece of kit that adds convenience and versatility like never before to your Awning set-up. If you don't have an Awning rail attachment on your vehicle, the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit is the ideal alternative for attaching your Awning tunnel to your vehicle without having a permanent attachment. Whether you're setting up camp for the weekend or embarking on a cross-country road trip, the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit provides a secure and stable connection, ensuring your awning stays firmly in place, even in windy conditions. Plus, its versatile design allows for quick and easy detachment whenever you're ready to hit the road again. With the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit, you'll spend less time setting up and more time enjoying the great outdoors. Whether you're lounging in the shade, hosting a barbecue, or simply relaxing with friends and family, this must-have accessory provides the perfect blend of convenience and reliability. Simply attach the magnetic strip to the top of your vehicle in the desired position, hold the cowl up to the other side of the strip and then thread the 'Figure of 8 Strips' onto the awning tunnel beading. Alternatively, thread the awning tunnel beading to the strip, before attaching to the roof of the vehicle. You can use the four bright guy lines provided to add extra security during windy weather by attaching them to the four loops on the magnetic strip, pegging the two end guy lines alongside your vehicle and then the other two over the top of your vehicle. Key features of the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit: 1x 270cm magnetic strip 4x Glow in the dark plastic pegs 3x Figure of 8 C channels 4x XL Bright guy lines to secure the Magnetic Drive-Away Kit to the side and the top of the vehicle 1x Carry Bag Recommended to use on vehicles of the height range 180cm - 240cm Not suitable for vehicles with roof bars Dimensions/Specifications: Length - 270cm Width - 17cm Depth - 1cm Pack size - 90cm x 25cm x 10cm Weight - 1kg Made from/with: Magnet Plastic Polyester
