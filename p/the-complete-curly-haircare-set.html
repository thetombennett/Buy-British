The ultimate. Our complete 5 Step Curly Haircare routine! Featuring our Bio-Active, Seaweed & Herbal Super Nutrient Curly Haircare — Hair & Scalp Oil, Curly Shampoo with Shampoo Brush, Curly Conditioner, Hair Protecting Hair Towel and our protecting/restructuring Nutri-Therm Hair Serum. Hair & Scalp Oil: A rebalancing pre-wash treatment. Infused with Broccoli Seed, Oat and Jojoba oils, plus botanical extracts and seaweed, it moisturises and strengthens hair while supporting healthy growth. Use by massaging into the scalp and hair, leave overnight or for 15-20 minutes before washing, or as a leave-in treatment for smoothing fly-aways. It leaves hair moisturised, soft and rejuvenated, while nourishing and rebalancing the scalp. Curly Shampoo & Conditioner: Our Super Nutrient Curly Shampoo and Conditioner are made with mineral-rich seaweed, herbs and curl-focused conditioning ingredients to gently cleanse, nourish, condition and define curls. Suitable for hair pattern types 1 to 4, from fine to textured hair and from thick to thin hair, they help reduce frizz, improve manageability, support shine and leave curls feeling soft, fresh and weightlessly cared for. The Shampoo Brush: Our soft, flexible, massaging shampoo brush is designed to gently promote scalp circulation, while evenly distributing shampoo for a deeper clean and healthier-feeling hair. It can also be used to distribute and massage the scalp with our Hair & Scalp Oil. The Hair Towel: This soft, lightweight towel reduces drying time, minimises breakage and frizz, and features a secure button closure. Quick-drying and easy to wash, it’s a gentle alternative to heavy cotton towels and ideal for curls. Nutri-Therm Hair Serum: Our unique silicone-free serum offers 13 benefits, including heat protection, restructuring and hydration. Infused with seaweed, bamboo and eclipta, it smooths, shines, revitalises and protects your hair post-wash, helping curls feel healthy, vibrant and more manageable. To use: Apply 2-8 pumps to damp hair through mid-lengths to ends, after washing and before drying. Less is more. Experience softer, healthier-looking and more defined curls with our 5 Step Super Nutrient Curly Haircare Set. Set contains: 1 x Super Nutrient Hair & Scalp Oil 1 x Super Nutrient Curly Shampoo 1 x Super Nutrient Curly Conditioner 1 x Nutri-Therm Hair Serum 1 x The Hair Towel 1 x Shampoo Brush
