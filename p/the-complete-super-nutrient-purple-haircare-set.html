The Complete Super Nutrient Purple Haircare Set A ritual to tone, cleanse, condition, nourish & protect, as well as brighten blonde, silver & grey hair with seaweed & botanicals. All silicone‑free, sulphate‑free, powered by wild Cornish seaweed. Complete natural, effective care & support of your scalp, and natural strengthening and nurturing of your hair. Purple Shampoo & Conditioner:A gentle toning duo formulated with organic seaweed, blue spirulina, alkanet root, and Cape jasmine fruit, along with other haircare botanicals. These plant‑based pigments help neutralise yellow and brassy tones while restoring softness, balance, and shine.How to use: To neutralise yellow or brassy tones, use our Purple Shampoo & Conditioner with every wash until your desired tone is reached. Then, use weekly or as needed to maintain brightness and balance, alternating with our regular Super Nutrient Shampoo & Conditioner.Add in our other amazing products in this set - for complete natural, effective care & strengthening of your hair and scalp: Super Nutrient Shampoo & Conditioner:Your everyday essentials — a balancing, sulphate‑free pair enriched with seaweed and botanicals. Together they hydrate, strengthen, and support scalp health while maintaining natural softness and vitality between purple treatments. Nutri‑Therm Hair Serum:Our award‑winning leave‑in treatment offers heat protection, hydration, and long‑lasting shine. With seaweed, bamboo, and eclipta, it smooths, strengthens, and seals moisture into each strand. Apply 2–8 pumps through mid‑lengths to ends after washing, before drying or styling. Super Nutrient Hair & Scalp Oil: A deeply nourishing pre-wash treatment designed to rebalance & soothe the scalp, and soften strands. Infused with seaweed, Broccoli Seed, Oat, and Jojoba oils, it supports moisture retention, strengthens hair, and encourages healthy growth. Use before shampooing — leave in for 20 minutes or overnight — or apply a small amount to dry hair to smooth and soften.Shampoo Brush: Flexible and gentle for massaging the scalp and evenly distributing products.
