Hanley Breeze Campervan Awning

The Hanley Breeze is an inflatable drive-away awning for campervans with an attachment height of 190cm to 250cm. It gives you a medium-sized living area: room to eat, sit out a wet afternoon or store kit, without committing to a full family footprint. The tunnel has a door on each side, so you can get into the van without walking through the living area, and a built-in porch gives you somewhere covered to leave boots and wet gear. A roof skylight and ventilation panels keep the inside bright, and the doors have flymesh panels for warm evenings. It is made from OLTECH REPRO 210T ripstop, the fabric OLPRO makes from recycled plastic bottles, with a 5,000mm hydrostatic head. Reinforced TPU Breeze beams pitch with the included manual pump. The AQUABARRIER zip system keeps water out of the tunnel base, and the TERRAGRIP peg system holds it steady on different ground. Dual 4mm and 6mm beading on the tunnel fits either standard rail size.
