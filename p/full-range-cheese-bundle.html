Get the full Appleby Creamery experience with this epic cheese bundle, giving you the chance to try every type of cheese they make in their small Cumbrian creamery.. You'll receive 8 cheeses totalling over 1.6kg, including:. 200g Old Applebian - a crumbly, open-textured cheese with a pronounced, lemony flavour and a lingering creamy aftertaste. Made from pasteurised cows milk to a traditional Westmorland recipe.. 230g Eden Valley Brie - a white mould-ripened brie cheese that's luxuriously creamy, with a rich buttery centre that's perfect for slathering on crusty bread. 230g Eden Smokie Brie - a smokie version of Eden Valley, smoked with a subtle mix of apple and oak wood to deliver a gentle smokiness that compliments the cheese's natural creaminess. 150g Black Dub Blue - a semi-soft blue veined cheese that strikes the perfect balance between creaminess and tang. 200g Flakebridge - a vibrant, tangy cheese based on Red Leicester. Creamy and close-textured with a well-rounded flavour that’s ideal for both cooking and your cheeseboard.. 200g Hootenanny - a delicate semi-hard goat’s cheese. Pure white with a rich, creamy texture and fresh, gentle flavours.. Every cheese is made from pasteurised milk and is suitable for vegetarians.
