This set brings together our complete Super Nutrient haircare range—rooted in organic seaweed, herbs, and nourishing botanicals—designed to support hair and scalp health from root to tip. Featuring our Hair & Scalp Oil, Shampoo + Shampoo Brush, Conditioner, Hair Towel, Nutri-Therm Hair Serum, and now our new Light Hold Hair Mousse for gentle, natural styling. Hair & Scalp Oil: A rebalancing pre-wash treatment enriched with Broccoli Seed, Oat, and Jojoba oils, plus botanical extracts and seaweed. It moisturises and strengthens hair while supporting a healthy scalp environment. Use as a pre-wash mask (leave for 15–20 mins or overnight), or sparingly as a leave-in to smooth ends and flyaways. Shampoo & Conditioner: Formulated with 100% natural ingredients including organic seaweed, Tuber Fleeceflower Root, Angelica Sinensis, Frankincense, and Mint Leaf. This duo gently cleanses, balances, and nourishes the scalp and hair without silicones, sulphates, or artificial fragrance. Suitable for all hair types. The Shampoo Brush: Soft and flexible, this brush promotes healthy scalp circulation while helping distribute product evenly. Ideal for use with shampoo or when massaging in the Hair & Scalp Oil. The Hair Towel: A lightweight, quick-drying towel that reduces frizz and breakage while gently securing hair with a button closure. A kind alternative to heavy cotton towels. Nutri-Therm Hair Serum: A lightweight, silicone-free serum that delivers 13 benefits—including hydration, heat protection, smoothing, and repair. With seaweed, bamboo, and eclipta, it helps revitalise and protect hair after washing. (To use: Apply 2–8 pumps to damp hair through mid-lengths to ends before drying.) Light Hold Hair Mousse: Our natural, aerosol-free mousse offers soft volume and light hold without stickiness. Seaweed, Aloe Vera, Broccoli Seed Oil, and Bamboo nourish and hydrate while enhancing natural texture. Ideal for curls, waves, and fine hair in need of lift. Experience full-spectrum care—from deep nourishment to gentle styling—with our complete hair wellness ritual.
