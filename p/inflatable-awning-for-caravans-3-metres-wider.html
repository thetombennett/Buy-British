Inflatable Awning for Caravans 3 Metres & Wider

The View 300 is an inflatable porch awning for caravans 235cm to 250cm tall. It gives you a panoramic living and storage space with 6 curtained windows and two tinted skylights, and it comes with a porch extension that zips onto either side of the awning: a useful spot for wet boots and kit before they reach the main space. The front panel has a u-shaped zip, so you can roll it open and use the awning as a veranda on dry days. With 5 doors in total, you can set the layout up however suits your pitch. It pitches on reinforced TPU air beams, with inflatable roof storm bars adding strength across the top, and the included manual pump does the inflating. The fabric is OLTECH REPRO 150D, made from repurposed single-use plastic bottles, with a 5,000mm hydrostatic head. Rubber awning ladder bands anchor it securely, bright storm straps are provided, and it packs into a heavy duty carry bag.
