Experience the luxury of a refreshing shower wherever you go with the OLPRO USB Battery Portable Shower. Designed for adventurers, festival-goers, campers, and outdoor enthusiasts, this innovative shower system brings convenience and comfort to your outdoor escapades like never before. Enjoy a revitalising shower after a day of hiking, camping or having a good time at a festival that'll have you feeling fresh and clean within minutes. Powered by a built-in rechargeable battery, this portable shower eliminates the need for traditional power sources. Simply charge up by USB ahead of your trip and rest assured you can look forward to multiple showers on a single charge (15 showers of 4 minutes each on one single charge, lasting a total of 60 minutes). It's eco-friendly and cost-effective, offering convenience without compromising on sustainability. Simply set up your shower in a shower tent or hang it from the tailgate of your vehicle and immerse the pump in a water source, such as a bucket of warm water or stream, and press the power button. The adjustable water flow allows you to customize your shower experience, while the ergonomic design ensures comfortable handling. A 10-litre bucket provides nearly 4 minutes of shower time. Designed for travel, this portable shower is compact and lightweight, making it easy to pack and transport no matter what your outdoor adventure entails. Whether you're backpacking through the wilderness or road-tripping across the country, this shower system is the perfect companion for maintaining hygiene on the move. Not only can it be used as a shower, but it's also great for washing dishes/clothes whilst camping, rinsing off sandy feet or paddleboards after a day at the beach, watering your plants in your garden and showering your furry friends on the go!
