Our Bio-Active, Natural, Seaweed & Herbal Super Nutrient Curly Shampoo is gentle, caring and uniquely formulated for curls, waves and textured hair. It provides your hair and scalp with the nourishment, hydration and gentle cleansing it needs to look and feel soft, fresh, healthy and beautifully defined. Pair it with our Super Nutrient Curly Conditioner. We make it using naturally derived ingredients, including mineral-rich seaweed and carefully selected herbs that are known for their hair and scalp-friendly properties. It is suitable for hair pattern types 1 to 4, from fine to textured hair and from thick to thin hair, making it ideal for anyone looking to gently cleanse, improve curl definition, reduce frizz and support healthier-feeling hair without build-up. It is free from silicones, SLS, parabens, artificial fragrances and colours, and is sulphate free. It is never tested on animals and is vegan friendly. We include our in-house extracts made from Seaweed, Tuber Fleeceflower Root, Angelica Sinensis Root, Frankincense Resin and Mint Leaf. We also use hair-loving plant oils, plant-based gentle cleansers, essential oils, curl-supporting conditioning ingredients and adjust it to a scalp and hair-friendly pH level. We’re sure you will love it as much as we do!
