The 3 Step Haircare Set + Detox A ritual to detox, cleanse, condition and protect Detox Shampoo for hair & scalp reset; Super Nutrient duo for everyday care Silicone‑free, sulphate‑free, powered by wild Cornish seaweed Discover our 3 Step Haircare + Detox Set — a simple routine that combines a deep cleansing Detox Shampoo with our daily Super Nutrient Shampoo, Conditioner, and Nutri‑Therm Hair Serum for lasting scalp and hair vitality. Detox Shampoo: A deeply cleansing yet protective shampoo designed to remove build‑up and impurities. Powered by wild Cornish seaweed & botanicals it gently lifts residue from conventional hair products while also softening and shielding strands. Perfect for the start of your natural haircare journey with us, or as a reset if your hair ever feels heavy or coated (e.g post salon visit). Super Nutrient Shampoo & Conditioner: Your regular routine. This gentle, sulphate‑free duo is enriched with organic seaweed & botanicals. Unlike the Detox Shampoo, which focuses on a thorough cleanse, the Super Nutrient Shampoo provides ongoing hydration and scalp balance, while the Conditioner restores softness, improves manageability, and reduces breakage. Together, they maintain healthy, vibrant hair without heaviness or residue. Nutri-Therm Hair Serum: Our unique, award winning, silicone-free serum offers 13 benefits, including heat protection, restructuring, and hydration. Infused with seaweed, bamboo, and eclipta, it smooths, shines, revitalises, and protects hair post-wash. To use: Apply 2–8 pumps to damp hair through mid-lengths to ends after washing and before drying. Less is more. The 3 Step Haircare Set + Detox is ideal for: Those seeking a complete natural haircare routine that begins with a clarifying reset and continues with gentle daily nourishment and protection.
