A soft-touch, lightweight and highly functional running top with the added bonus of being part of the REFLECT360 range to give you that extra reflectivity when out in low light conditions. Manufactured using 125gr interlock polyester gives you excellent moisture transportation away from your body. This helps to ensure the top does not stick to your body and remains free to move as you do. The material has a 'soft touch' feel about it for that added bit of luxury! Stylish reflective print has been strategically placed on the shoulders/arms to give you a great loop and high levels of multi-directional reflectivity - a must have if you are out in the early mornings or evenings. The REFLECT360 detail helps reflect vehicle lights and makes it easier for a driver to identify your position further in advance.
