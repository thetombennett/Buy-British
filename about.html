About BuyBritish

Our mission

BuyBritish helps consumers discover British retailers, British brands and UK-made products.

We want to make it easier to find products, compare prices and make informed decisions about where you shop.

What we mean by British

British businesses and products can have different connections to the UK. We aim to make those distinctions clear, including UK-headquartered retailers, British brands, and products designed, assembled or manufactured in the UK.

What we're building

BuyBritish is in development. We're working towards a platform that helps shoppers discover products and compare offers from retailers in one place.
