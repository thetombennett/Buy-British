About BuyBritish

Our mission

BuyBritish helps consumers discover British retailers, British brands and UK-made products.

We believe shoppers should be able to easily source the best of british products, compare prices and make informed decisions about the businesses they choose to support.

What we mean by British

British businesses and products can have different connections to the UK. A brand may be British-owned or headquartered here, while a product may be designed, assembled or manufactured in the UK.

We want to make these distinctions clear, helping shoppers understand what sits behind the products they discover.

What we're building

We're developing BuyBritish to make it easier to discover products from British businesses, explore available options and compare prices from retailers.

We're at the beginning of that journey, and we're working towards bringing the platform to life.
